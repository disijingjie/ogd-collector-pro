Research Overview

This doctoral research develops a comprehensive performance evaluation framework for open government data (OGD) platforms, addressing the critical gap between data supply and utilization effectiveness in China's rapidly evolving OGD ecosystem.

The 4E Framework

Our novel 4E framework extends the traditional 3E model (Economy-Efficiency-Effectiveness) by incorporating Equity as a critical fourth dimension.

📦

E1 Supply Assurance

8 indicators measuring data availability, coverage, and update quality

🛠️

E2 Platform Service

7 indicators evaluating accessibility, functionality, and interaction support

✅

E3 Data Quality

7 indicators assessing accuracy, standardization, and usability

📊

E4 Utilization Effect

6 indicators capturing download volume, applications, and developer activity

⚖️

E5 Equity

Supplementary dimension for accessibility and disadvantaged group support

🔬

Combined Weights

AHP + Entropy method for objective-subjective weight integration

Methodology

A five-method mixed approach ensuring robustness and complementary insights:

🎯

TOPSIS

Comprehensive performance ranking with relative closeness to ideal solution

📐

DEA-BCC

Technical efficiency analysis under variable returns to scale

🔗

DEMATEL

Causal relationship mapping between influencing factors

🔀

fsQCA

Configurational analysis of equifinal paths to high performance

📈

Multi-period DID

Policy effect estimation with event study design

Key Findings

Data Caliber Illusion

Raw dataset counts overstate supply by 15-40% due to inconsistent counting methodologies across platforms.

"Iron Triangle" Necessary Conditions

fsQCA identifies E2, E3, and E4 as necessary conditions (consistency=1.00) for high performance.

Quality-Effect Gap

National average E3=0.832 but E4=0.268, revealing a critical quality-effect utilization gap.

Policy Effect with Lag

"Data 20 Articles" policy shows significant positive effect (β=0.043, p<0.01) with 1-year lag.
